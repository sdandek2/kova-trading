📡 Regime Detection

BULL

SPY above MA200 + MA20 > MA50
VIX normal / low
Market breadth > 50%

Actions: Full-size longs, pyramid up to 2×, partial exits on high confidence
Capital multiplier: 1.0–1.1×

CHOP

Mixed signals — no clear trend
VIX medium
Breadth 35–55%

Actions: Higher score bar (≥60), smaller size, no leveraged ETF pyramids
Capital multiplier: 0.6×

BEAR

SPY below MA200
VIX high / extreme
Breadth < 35%

Actions: Shorts + inverse ETFs (SQQQ/SOXS), longs blocked, min score raised
Capital multiplier: 0.4–0.5×

🛡 Risk Rules

Circuit Breaker

−4%

Daily P&L loss halts all trading for the rest of the day

Stop Loss

4%

Default hard stop. ATR trailing stop activates once in profit

Take Profit

8–9%

Claude sets per trade. Partial exit at TP on high-confidence positions

Max Positions

21

Configurable from iOS app. Sector cap: max 5 per sector

Position Size

15%

Max per position. Penny stocks capped at 3%

Pyramid Max

2×

Max add-ons per winner. Each add ≤40% remaining cash. Hard limit.

EOD Flat

3:45 PM

All positions closed. New entries blocked. No overnight gaps.

Gap-Down Exit

−2%

In first 25 minutes: gap >2% down → exit immediately

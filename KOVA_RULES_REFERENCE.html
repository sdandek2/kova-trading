Regime Score (−3 to +3)

Internal score used to classify regime. Combines SPY trend, VIX level, and market breadth (% of universe above MA20).

  • Positive scores → bull
  • Near-zero → chop
  • Negative → bear
  • Confidence 0.0–1.0 output per regime
🏦 Leveraged ETF Rules
  • Allowed ONLY when regime = bull AND vix ≠ high/extreme
  • In Aggressive mode: up to 2 correlated leveraged ETFs simultaneously
  • Long leveraged: TQQQ SOXL TECL FNGU SPXL UPRO UDOW TNA
  • Short leveraged: SQQQ SOXS TECS FNGS SPXS SPXU SDOW TZA
  • Pyramiding leveraged ETFs also blocked when regime ≠ bull

📌 Hard Limits
🔴
Max per stock: 10% of portfolioHard cap enforced after all multipliers
🔴
Penny stocks (<$5): 3% maxPrevents huge share counts on cheap stocks
🔴
Per-cycle max: 5 new tradesExits and trailing stops never blocked by this
🟡
Pyramid spend: ≤40% of cashPer pyramid add, never overcommit cash
📊 ATR Stop Sizing
  • Regular stocks: stop = entry − 1.5 × ATR
  • Leveraged ETFs: stop = entry − 2.0 × ATR
  • Watermarks tracked in-memory and restored after Railway restart
  • Default trailing stop fallback: 4% (Claude overrides per trade)
  • Default take profit fallback: 20% (Claude overrides per trade)

🛡️ Risk Controls & Circuit Breakers

🔴 Daily Loss Circuit Breaker

If portfolio P&L drops more than 4% in a single day:

  • All new BUY and SHORT entries blocked
  • Exits, covers, and trailing stops still execute
  • Logged to circuit_breaker_log
  • Resets next trading day
⏱️ Cycle Interval

Default: 600 seconds (10 min). Configurable.

  • 300s = 5-min cycles
  • 600s = 10-min cycles (default)
  • Changed via risk settings
  • Stops, exits, and trailing stop checks run every cycle regardless
🚨 Blocked Trade Tracking

When a trade is blocked (not risk-rejected, just guarded):

  • Logged to blocked_trade_log
  • Price tracked at +15min, +1h, +EOD
  • Used to evaluate what was missed
📊 Near-Miss Tracking
  • Candidates scoring 35–44 are logged to near_miss_log
  • Price followed up at +15min, +1h, and EOD
  • Used in Sprint Review to evaluate if threshold is too high

⚖️ Signal Weight Auto-Tuning

Bidirectional Weight Adjustment (Sundays, after 60 days)

For each signal (options flow, insider, dark pool, etc.) with ≥ 15 trades in history:

Win RateDirectionAdjustmentLimit
< 40%Signal is underperforming−15% weightFloor: 50% of default
40–70%Normal performanceNo change—
> 70%Signal is outperforming+15% weightCap: 150% of default
Example: Barchart Call Flow
  • Default weight: 100%
  • After 60 days: 35% win rate → weight drops to 85%
  • Continues drifting down if <40%: 85% → 70% → floor 50%
  • Rebounds if win rate recovers >70%: floor → 65% → 80% → cap 150%
What Gets Tracked
  • signal_performance_log table: signal type, symbol, outcome, P&L
  • Win = trade was profitable at exit
  • Per-signal win rate computed over rolling window
  • Weights stored in signal_weights table
  • Applied to scoring on next cycle after update
